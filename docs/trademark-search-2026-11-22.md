# Name search for "optdoc" (22 November 2026)

A collision check made before the name was chosen. It is not a professional trademark search and no legal
opinion. Names of private persons who hold or applied for marks are left out.

## Method

- **Domains**: DNS (NS and SOA) for `.com`, `.de`, `.org`, `.net`, `.eu`, `.app`, `.io`, `.it`.
- **Package names**: PyPI, npm, crates.io.
- **Code**: GitHub repository search.
- **Web**: search for "optdoc" and close variants.
- **DPMAregister** (German, EU and international marks designating DE or the EU), wildcard search, with the
  control query `?meddra?` returning its known 4 hits: `?optdoc?`, `?optdok?`, `?opt?doc`.
- **TMview** (all connected offices, including the USPTO and the Italian office): fuzzy search `optdoc`,
  "contains" search `optdoc`, fuzzy search `optdok`. Every answer was checked to have come from the service
  (response size), not left over from an earlier query.

## Findings

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Domains | `.org`, `.de`, `.eu`, `.app`, `.io` unregistered; `.com` registered without a site; `.net` "Coming Soon" |
| Packages | `optdoc` free on PyPI, npm and crates.io |
| GitHub | two small repositories: "OptDoc – Optimize Document" (2022, one star) and "OptDocs" (documentation on option pricing) |
| Web | "OptDoc for Python", a module for documenting command-line options (SourceForge, last updated 2013); "Opt.Doc", shared medical and dental offices in São Paulo (`optdoc.com.br`). No program for document clean-up or OCR. |
| DPMAregister | `optdoc`, `optdok`: no mark |
| TMview | no mark "optdoc" in any office; no mark containing "optdoc"; `optdok`: only lapsed or unrelated marks |

Neighbours that are alive in classes 9 or 42:

- **OPT2DOC**, German application of 5 November 2026, classes 9 and 42, filed by a private applicant through
  patent attorneys; the list of goods was not yet published. To be watched.
- **PTDoc**, EU mark (IT security firm), class 9 "software"; contained in "optdoc" in writing, different in sound.
- **OPTIDOCK**, EU word mark, classes 9 and 42 (software); **OptimiDoc**, EU word and figurative mark,
  class 9 (software for document capture and OCR). Both are close to "optidoc" (below), farther from "optdoc".

## The candidate before: "optidoc"

Rejected on 22 November 2026. OPTIDOCK (EU, classes 9 and 42) sounds the same as "optidoc"; OptimiDoc
(EU, class 9) sells document capture with OCR; several products are called OptiDoc, among them an
open-source Android app that photographs documents, finds their edges and reads them by OCR, and a US
document-imaging company that holds `optidoc.com`.

## Earlier candidates

Checked the same way and rejected the same day: Leggio, Recto (an open-source "PDF viewer, OCR tool and
document converter" of that name), Explico, Explana, Stiro, Aplanat, Evolvo, Legibel, Chiaro, Lucido.
Spianato passed; Spiana and Leggibile passed with reservations.

## Assessment

"optdoc" is free as a mark, as a package name and as a product name in its field. It is weak as a mark (it
describes what it does) and will not be registered; it names a free AGPL tool. The OPT2DOC application is the
one to look at again once its list of goods is published.
